# Hotel guide research

Checked 28 September 2026 for the November 2026 trip. The source-linked, user-facing shortlist is `/hotels/`; structured entries are in `src/assets/js/data/hotels.js`.

The guide compares 11 properties: Amber, Adelphi and Avani in Pattaya; Clover, Indigo and Courtyard in Patong; Clover Asoke, Mercure Sukhumvit 11, Solaria, Carlton and Grande Centre Point Terminal 21 in Bangkok. All are listed as four- or five-star properties. Star class is distinct from Booking.com guest scores (8.6–9.3/10) and review counts, which are dated snapshots rather than live data.

The first options to price are Amber, Clover Patong and Clover Asoke. They combine good guest feedback with useful locations. Carlton is the strongest-reviewed Bangkok upgrade. These are recommendations to compare, not claims of the cheapest available November rate. Individual recent noise or maintenance comments are used as room-selection cautions, not as proof of a general defect.

The group may be five or six adults. Three rooms are the simple baseline: three twins for six, or two twins plus a single-occupied room for five. Two rooms require one or two genuine triple arrangements. Clover Asoke and Grande Centre Point list three-single-bed categories; Avani has published three-adult capacity in selected categories; Indigo and Carlton advertise extra beds with conditions. None of that establishes date-specific inventory or an included third bed. Solaria Twin's two-adult-plus-child limit must not be mistaken for three-adult occupancy.

Arrival in Pattaya after midnight on 14 November requires reserving the preceding 13 November night and notifying the hotel of late arrival. Bangkok's six-night plan retains the 26 November night for late departures. Reducing rooms after Moe leaves or substituting day-use depends on actual occupancy and written hotel confirmation; the calculator does not silently assume either saving.

## What is verified and what is not

Property pages, official room/location details, aggregate guest reviews and selected recent review cautions were checked. Each guide card links to its official site, guest reviews and relevant room details. Walking times are approximate planning judgments based on location, not measured journeys.

Public booking pages did not provide a usable November group quote through the available research tools. There are **no verified November rates, availability guarantees or reservations**. Dated booking links carry each city's check-in/out plus the currently selected five/six adults and two/three rooms; the booking site may require rechecking these filters and bed types.

All cost inputs are explicitly illustrative USD amounts, per room per night. The example starts at $100 per room and a $50 extra-adult supplement. Those figures are not attributed to a hotel. The calculation is rooms × nights × room rate plus any extra-adult supplements, divided equally by the selected group size. The same rooms are held for all 14 nights; the example excludes tax/service, breakfast and other unentered charges. Compare complete, cancellation-equivalent quotes before choosing.

No hotel credit, card benefit or points valuation is used. Legacy settings remain in the database for compatibility, but the obsolete calculator and booking assumptions were removed from the interface.
