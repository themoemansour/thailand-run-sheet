Lost or stolen passport

Annoying, fixable, and it takes a few days. Start the same day.

  1. Police report. Tourist Police (1155) or the nearest station. You need the written report for everything after this.
  2. US Embassy, American Citizen Services. Book an emergency passport. Bring the police report, a passport photo and any ID or photo of the old passport.
  3. Thai Immigration. A new passport has no Thai entry stamp. Ask the embassy what to take to Immigration to get your entry recorded before you fly out.
  4. Tell the airline and the insurer. The flight home may need re-issuing under the new passport number.

Cards, phone, cash

The phone is the thing most likely to go, so none of this should live only on it.

  1. Freeze the card in the bank app, then call the number on the back of a card that still works. Save each bank's international number before you fly.
  2. Lost phone: borrow one and sign in to Find My / Find My Device. Your eSIM or Thai SIM can be reissued at a True or AIS shop with your passport.
  3. Everyone carries a second card in a different place from the first, plus a small USD stash.
