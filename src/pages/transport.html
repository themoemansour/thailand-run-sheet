Bangkok

Trains first, apps second, taxis last.

BTS / MRT17–60฿
Grab or Bolt hop80–250฿
Airport → Sukhumvit, metered240–310฿
Expressway tolls, extra50–75฿
Airport Rail Link15–45฿

Get a Rabbit card on day one. Sukhumvit traffic is immobile 17:00–19:00 and the train always wins. If a driver says the meter is broken, get out — there are thousands more.

Phuket

Most expensive island transport in Thailand, by design.

Airport → Patong, fixed taxi800–1,000฿
Same run on Bolt400–500฿
Patong → Kata, Grab250–400฿
Patong → Kata, tuk-tuk400–600฿
Songthaew, 06:00–18:0030–100฿

Phuket tuk-tuks run an unofficial cartel rate, 40–60% over the app price for the same ten kilometres. Grab has a sanctioned green-roofed pickup zone at the airport. Staying in Patong means most nights you walk, which is the main reason it's worth being there.

Pattaya & scooters

Read the law section first.

Songthaew along Beach Rd10–20฿

Checkpoint fine, no IDP500–2,000฿
Insurance if unlicensedVoid

Baht buses (songthaews) run fixed loops along Beach and Second Road for ฿10–20 — hop on, ring the bell, pay at the window. Never hand over your passport as a scooter deposit, and photograph the whole bike before riding off: the damage-claim shakedown on return is standard.
