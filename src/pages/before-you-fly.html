The exemption was halved on Sep 15, 2026 — Americans used to get 60 days, now 30. Yours fits comfortably. File at tdac.immigration.go.th and nowhere else: it is free, and every site charging for it is a scam. The proposed ฿300 arrival fee still isn't in force, but budget for it in case.
